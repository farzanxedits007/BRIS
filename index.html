Sigillum
Chapter I

The Charter & Institutional Nexus

BIRS stands as a society of record under the Department of Business & Management, uniting academic precision with corporate innovation.

Founded in Purpose. The Business Research & Innovation Society was chartered to serve as the intellectual bridge between engineering exactitude and the applied arts of commerce.

Mission. To cultivate empirical research, symposium discourse, pitch enterprise, and student-led leadership across the KSK campus and beyond.

Affiliation. Operated under the patronage of the Department of Management Sciences, University of Engineering & Technology, Kala Shah Kaku Campus.
